## Short description (paste in #contest-submissions)

A delivery drone rips through a dusk city at absurd speed, rings the doorbell and drops its box in 0.8 seconds. The customer opens it: empty. One-star review. Meanwhile, the pizza is still sitting in the warehouse. Speed was never the problem — contents not included.

Interpretation of the theme "Speed": speed as a promise. When delivery is only judged by how fast it arrives, the one thing nobody checks is whether anything arrived at all.

## AI & third-party disclosure
- The entire film is generated by a Blender Python script (bpy 5.2, EEVEE). Every object, material, camera move and animation curve is created in code. No downloaded models, textures, HDRIs or animation clips.
- The script and the sound-synthesis code were written with the help of Claude (Anthropic AI) under my direction. I chose the concept, story and tuning.
- Audio is 100 % synthesized in Python (numpy/scipy): music bed, rotor hum, whooshes, doorbell, wah-wah. No samples or stock music.
- Only third-party asset: the Inter typeface (SIL Open Font License) for on-screen text.
- Final grade (glow, vignette, grain, encode) done with ffmpeg.

## Workflow notes (Procedural award)
1. **City:** a street path is built from straight segments plus circular fillets; buildings are placed along it from a seeded random generator (height, width, depth, colour). Window lights are a node-built material (fract/step math on object coordinates + white noise per cell), so no textures exist.
2. **Camera and drone:** the drone follows the path by arc-length lookup; four cameras (chase, side, front, high) are tied to the same path. Braking is an exponential ease; the doorbell nudges and box drop are analytic.
3. **Character:** a two-bone IK solver in Python poses the receiver's arms; lean, crouch, brow and mouth are parameters driven by frame number.
4. **HUD and text:** speed readout, review stars and end card are generated text objects parented to the camera.
5. **Sound:** each sound is a formula (sawtooth/sine/noise through filters, envelopes and short delays).
6. Render: EEVEE, 12 samples, 360 frames; one script re-creates the whole film from nothing.
